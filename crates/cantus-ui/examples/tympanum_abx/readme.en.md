# Listening test: drums

Thank you for taking it. It is single drum hits (kick, snare, toms, hi-hat, cymbals) and short
grooves, each sounded three different ways; you will say whether you can tell two of them apart
and which one sounds better to you. There are no right answers about taste, and if you cannot
tell something apart, that is a result too.

**It takes about 20–25 minutes** (about 12 more for the extra part, which is optional). You can
stop whenever you like: the answers stay in the browser, and when you open the page again in the
same browser you carry on where you left off.

## How to take it

1. Unzip the whole folder and open `index.html` with a double click (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or
   Safari). It needs no internet.
2. Use headphones or good speakers in a quiet place. Set the volume on the first clip and leave
   it. The clips of one question have the same loudness and the same length.
3. Answer the short questions about you (only the first is required) and press *Start*.
4. **Part 1** — 24 trials. A and B are two different sounds; X is one of them, at random. Listen
   as much as you like (keys `A`, `B`, `X`; space to stop; `L` to loop) and say which one X is.
5. **Part 2** — 26 questions. Two clips of the same thing: say which sounds more like a real
   acoustic drum kit, well recorded, or that you have no preference. A word on why helps a lot.
6. At the end you see your results and which sound was which. Press **Download results (JSON)**
   and send that file to whoever gave you the test.

*← Previous* goes back to change an answer. *Start again* (in the results) erases everything.
