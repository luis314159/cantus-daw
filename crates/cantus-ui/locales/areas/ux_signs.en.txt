# What drives a control and how to take it back ({menu} is the menu entry's own label).

ux.drive.clip.back = Right-click → {menu}, or delete the clip

ux.drive.mod.back = Alt+drag sets the depth; right-click → {menu} disconnects

ux.drive.vca.back = Right-click the strip → {menu} to release it
