# The prompt "Master for streaming".
listen.prompt.master.title = Master for streaming
listen.prompt.master.description = Master the open song for a streaming platform with Cantus's own effects, and check the result by listening.
listen.prompt.master.platform = Where it is going: spotify, apple_music, youtube, tidal, amazon_music, soundcloud, cd, club or broadcast (Spotify if not said).
listen.prompt.master.text = Master this song for {platform} ({lufs} LUFS integrated, true peak at or under {ceiling} dBTP). First call audition on the whole song with master false to hear the mix as it reaches the master, and look at its loudness, range, spectrum, correlation and clicks; fix in the mix what a master cannot (a track masking another — masking_matrix — or a click at a section change). Then call master_assist with platform "{id}" (and curve set to the song's genre, or reference to a reference track in the project's folder), read the proposed chain and why each unit is there, and apply it with apply_master_chain (or replace_master_chain when it replaces the master's own effects: it asks the user first). Finally call audition again and confirm the integrated loudness is within 0.5 LU of {lufs} and the true peak at or under {ceiling} dBTP; explain in plain words what the master changed.
