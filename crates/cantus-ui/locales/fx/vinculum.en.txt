vinculum.tip.model = The circuit: a console's VCA (feed-forward, its ratio, attack and release stepped), a photocell leveller (the output lights a panel that dims the input) or a valve variable-mu (the ratio rises as it works).
vinculum.tip.ratio = The VCA's ratio: 2:1 glues, 4:1 holds, 10:1 limits.
vinculum.tip.att = The VCA's attack: how soon it answers a hit. 10 or 30 ms let a drum's first crack through.
vinculum.tip.rel = The VCA's release; Auto lets a lone hit go at 0.1 s and sustained work slowly, so the mix breathes without pumping.
vinculum.tip.char = Compress works softly; Limit holds the level firmly (the opto near 10:1, the vari-mu from 4:1 rising to 20:1).
vinculum.tip.time = The vari-mu's six time positions: attack and release; 5 and 6 depend on the programme (a lone peak comes back fast, a long passage slowly).
vinculum.tip.key_lamp = What the detector hears. Click to choose: its own input, or any strip of the desk as the key (in another DAW, the plug-in's sidechain input).
vinculum.tip.auto_lamp = Auto release: lit, a lone hit lets go at 0.1 s and sustained work slowly. Click to light it; click again for the release it had.
vinculum.tip.colour_lamp = Lit while the valve colour is up: odd harmonics from the valves, more as they work. Set it on the Valve card.
