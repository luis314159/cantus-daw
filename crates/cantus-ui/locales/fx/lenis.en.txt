lenis.tip.qual = The analysis frame: Fast hears sooner with coarser bins, Precise resolves the low mids and is later. A change starts the analysis again (a gap of one latency).
lenis.tip.curve = Soft barely touches a peak just over the threshold; Hard takes every peak by its share as soon as it stands out.
lenis.tip.chan = Linked hears both channels as one; Independent treats each on its own; Mid and side treats the middle and the sides apart.
lenis.tip.delta = Hear only what is taken away: the rings and whistles, never the music.
lenis.tip.gate = Drag the focus's limit: nothing is touched beyond it (a third of an octave's fade).
lenis.tip.node = Drag a band: left and right its frequency, up and down its amount; the wheel sets its width. Double-click switches it on or off.
