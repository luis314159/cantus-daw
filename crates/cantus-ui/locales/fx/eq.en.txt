eq.hint = Double-click the display to add a band — at the edges for a cut or a shelf, at the floor for a notch — or press on the curve and pull. Drag a dot to move it (every selected dot moves with it), drag the background to select several, the wheel for Q, Ctrl+wheel for gain, Shift for fine, Alt to lock the axis, Alt+click to bypass, double-click a dot to type values, right-click for the band's menu. Keys: 1–9 select, arrows nudge, [ ] change Q, Delete removes, S auditions.
