# University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (MIS): cello and double bass

- **URL:** https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html (cello and bass pages, `sound files/MIS/Strings/`)
- **Files live at:** `$CANTUS_REFS\arcus\iowa\` (fetched 2026-11-22 for the
  `arcus` builder; not duplicated here). `cello_list.txt` / `bass_list.txt` list the site's files;
  `selected.txt` lists what was taken.
- **Access date:** 2026-11-22 (files); the terms were re-read for this study on 2026-11-23.
- **Licence, verbatim (MIS.html, as recorded in `research/instruments-next/strings-verified.md` §2.4):**
  > may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions
  Not a Creative Commons licence.
- **Content:** 39 cello files, 35 bass files, mono 16-bit 44.1 kHz AIFF. Each file is a
  chromatic run on one string (e.g. `Cello.arco.mf.sulD.D3B3.aiff`), notes separated by silence.
  Arco and pizzicato, pp / mf / ff, every string. Anechoic chamber, Neumann KM 84 at about 5 ft
  (stated for the violin, the same layout expected). One instrument per family; the player and
  the make are not stated. **No vibrato** (measured: extent under 1 cent on every arco note).
- **Use:** measurement — yes, and the best for the body fit (no room). Shipping: "without
  restrictions" covers it in plain words, but it is not a standard licence: *lawyer* before a
  pack. Credit: University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios.
