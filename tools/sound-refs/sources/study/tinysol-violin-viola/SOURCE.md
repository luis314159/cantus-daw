# TinySOL (IRCAM Studio On Line subset): violin and viola, ordinario

- **URL:** https://zenodo.org/records/3685367 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.3685367), version 6.0 (Feb 2020)
- **How it was taken:** `TinySOL.tar.gz` (1 026 917 185 bytes) is a gzip tar, so it cannot be
  range-read. It was streamed once through `tar`, which wrote only `Strings/Violin/*` and
  `Strings/Viola/*`. The MD5 of the whole stream, `36030a7fe389da86c3419e5ee48e3b7f`, matches
  Zenodo. `TinySOL_metadata.csv` was also taken; its MD5, `a86c9bb115f69e61f2f25872e397fc4a`,
  matches.
- **Access date:** 2026-11-24
- **Licence:** the Zenodo licence field is `cc-by-4.0`. The record says, verbatim:

  > TinySOL can be used for creative purposes insofar at the use complies with the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license

## Content

- **Recording:** IRCAM, Paris, 1996-1999. Players and instruments are not named. They are
  independent of the Iowa, VSCO and Philharmonia instruments.
- **Format:** WAV, mono, 16-bit, 44.1 kHz, 2 to 10 s per note. All 593 files begin with `RIFF`.
- **Names:** `<Instr>-ord-<pitch>-<dyn>-<string>c-<misc>.wav`. The string number counts from
  1, the highest open string.
- **`audio/Strings/Violin/ordinario/`:** 284 files, 186 MB. 95 pp, 95 mf, 94 ff. Strings:
  1c 75, 2c 74, 3c 69, 4c 66.
- **`audio/Strings/Viola/ordinario/`:** 309 files, 196 MB. 103 each at pp, mf and ff.
  Strings: 1c 84, 2c 75, 3c 75, 4c 75.
- **Articulation:** sustained arco ordinario only. There is no pizzicato and there are no
  phrases.

## Verdict

**May ship, with attribution** (Cella, Ghisi, Lostanlen, Lévy, Fineberg, Maresz; IRCAM). Analysis
is allowed.
