# University of Iowa MIS: violin and viola (pre-2012 anechoic set)

- **URLs:**
  - https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISviolin.html
  - https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISviola.html

  The files are linked as `https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/sound files/MIS/Strings/{violin,viola}/<name>.aiff`.
  The index pages are saved in `meta/`, with the file lists `meta/MISviolin_list.txt` and
  `meta/MISviola_list.txt`.
- **Access date:** 2026-11-24 (the request was dated 2026-11-23).
- **Terms, verbatim** (`meta/MIS.html`):

  > Since 1997, these recordings have been freely available on this website and may be downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions.

## Content

- **Recording:** an anechoic chamber with one Neumann KM 84. MIS.html says:

  > Until 2011, the recordings were made with single Neumann KM 84 cardioid condenser microphone at 16/44.1.
- **Instruments and players:** not named on the pages. They are a different violin and viola from
  everything else here.
- **Format:** AIFF (FORM/AIFF), mono, 16-bit, 44.1 kHz. Each file is a chromatic run played
  note by note on one string.
- **`violin/`:** 71 files, 354 MB.

  | | pp | mf | ff |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | arco | 12 | 12 | 11 |
  | pizz | 11 | 13 | 12 |

  Every string is covered (sulG 18, sulD 16, sulA 18, sulE 19). The names look like
  `Violin.arco.pp.sulG.G3B3.aiff`.
- **`viola/`:** 62 files, 158 MB.

  | | pp | mf | ff |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | arco | 10 | 11 | 11 |
  | pizz | 10 | 10 | 10 |

  Every string is covered (C, G, D, A). The names look like `Viola.arco.sulC.pp.C3B3.aiff`;
  note that the string comes before the dynamic here.
- **Verification:** all 133 files begin with `FORM`.
- **Not taken:** the 2012 violin and viola sets (`MISviolin2012.html`), which are optional, to
  save disk.

## Verdict

"Without restrictions" is not a CC licence but grants unrestricted use. Treat it like the Iowa
cello and bass already in hand. **Analysis is allowed.** Shipping would rest on that
"without restrictions" wording (it is not CC0 or CC BY); I would keep it as measurement unless
the owner accepts it.
