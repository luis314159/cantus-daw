# Philharmonia Orchestra sound samples: violin and viola

- **URLs:**
  - Page: https://philharmonia.co.uk/audio-sound-samples/
  - Archive: https://philharmonia-assets.s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/2020/02/12112005/Strings.zip

  Only the `Strings/violin/*.mp3` and `Strings/viola/*.mp3` entries were taken, by range
  requests with CRC-32 checked. The nested `violin.zip` and `viola.zip` duplicates were left out.
  The cello and bass are in `../philharmonia/`.
- **Access date:** 2026-11-24

## Terms, verbatim (official page)

> You are free to use these samples as you wish, including releasing them as part of a commercial work.
>
> The only restriction is that they must not be sold or made available 'as is' (i.e. as samples or as a sampler instrument).

A GitHub mirror claims CC BY-SA 3.0. The official page does not say this.

## Content

- **Players:** Philharmonia players. Players and instruments are not named.
- **Format:** MP3 (ID3), mono, lossy.
- **Names:** `<instr>_<note>_<length|phrase>_<dynamic>_<articulation>.mp3`.
- **`raw/Strings/violin/`:** 1 502 files, 29 MB, from G3 up.
  - arco-normal: ff 182, f 179, mf 145, pp 137, p 119, mp 83.
  - Also molto-vibrato, non-vibrato, staccato, spiccato, au talon, harmonics and more.
  - **253 phrase files**: legato 37, spiccato 36, tremolo 31, staccato 28, glissando 28, trill 21, martelé 19, tenuto 13, detaché 13, au talon 10, punta d'arco 9, and others. Vibrato appears only as an attribute of the sustained notes, not as a phrase.
  - **71 pizz files**.
- **`raw/Strings/viola/`:** 973 files, 23 MB, from C3 up. `viola_D6_05_piano_arco-normal.mp3` is 0 bytes in the source zip itself, so it was deleted.
  - arco-normal: ff 183, pp 169, p 158, f 105, mp 88.
  - Also molto-vibrato, non-vibrato, trills, harmonics and glissando.
  - **55 phrase files**: legato 11, staccato 10, spiccato 9, detaché 9, tremolo 6, au talon 4, and one each of tasto, ponticello, con sordino and col legno.
  - **58 pizz files**.
