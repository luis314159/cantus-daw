## Licence

"Creative Commons 0" (CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication), as shown on each sound page.

## Content

- **cms4f, cello:** 10 improvisations around the note C ("Recording of a Cello improvising with
  the note C").
- **smoseson, cello:** bowed open strings A3, D3, G2 and C2, plus one chord ("Bowed note on
  cello").
- **pjcohen, upright bass:** 12 sounds, pizzicato ("Normal") on G1 to G#3 and F4, finger-stopped
  notes, and Bartók pizzicato. The page says: "sampled using a direct pickup as well as stereo
  overhead mics".
- **giac.biagi, gut-strung double bass:** one sound, pizzicato near the bridge.
- **Independence:** four different players and instruments, none already in hand.
- **Format:** MP3 preview (about 128 kbit/s). 28 files, 14 MB.

## Verdict

The licence is CC0, so these **may ship**, but only the originals, which need a Freesound login.
The previews here are lossy; use them for **listening and coarse measurement only**.
**Analysis is allowed.**

## Not taken

The PhonosUPF "violoncello …" sounds are CC0, but their pages say "violoncello processed
samples remix" or "high pitch transformation", so they are not clean recordings. The
sgossner "Solo Contrabass" sounds are VSCO 2, which is already in hand.
