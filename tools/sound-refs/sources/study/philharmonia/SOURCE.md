# Philharmonia Orchestra sound samples: cello and double bass

- **URL (page):** https://philharmonia.co.uk/audio-sound-samples/
- **URL (archive):** https://philharmonia-assets.s3-eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/2020/02/12112005/Strings.zip
  (171 467 548 bytes, Last-Modified 2020-02-12). Only the `Strings/cello/*.mp3` and
  `Strings/double bass/*.mp3` entries were taken, by HTTP range requests on the zip (each
  entry's CRC-32 was checked). The violin, viola, guitar, banjo and mandolin entries, the nested
  `cello.zip` and `double bass.zip` copies and a Dreamweaver `_notes/dwsync.xml` were left out.
- **Access date:** 2026-11-23

## Content

- **Instruments and players:** Philharmonia Orchestra players. The page names no player and no
  instrument. These are a different physical cello and bass from anything already in hand.
- **Format:** MP3 (ID3 header), mono. Lossy.
- **Files:** 1 741 in total.
  - `raw/Strings/cello/`: 889 files, 25 MB, C2 to C7.
  - `raw/Strings/double bass/`: 852 files, 17 MB, C1 to D4.
- **File names:** `<instrument>_<note>_<length>_<dynamic>_<articulation>.mp3`. The length is
  025, 05, 1 or 15 (0.25 to 1.5 s), `long`, or `phrase`.
- **Arco sustains:** arco-normal at pp, p, mp, mf (bass only), f and ff, and bass also at
  "molto-pianissimo", at every semitone. There are also non-vibrato, molto-vibrato, trills,
  harmonics and col legno.
- **Phrases:** 64 cello and 74 bass. They cover legato, detaché, staccato, spiccato, martelé and
  portato (bass), tenuto (bass), au talon, tremolo, glissando, sul tasto, sul ponticello,
  con sordino, molto vibrato and non vibrato, and cresc-decresc swells.
- **Pizzicato:** 3 cello files (pizz p and ff, snap-pizz) and 19 bass files (pizz at several
  notes, pizz tremolo, glissando, snap).

## Verdict

**Measurement only.** These may not ship. The terms are a custom licence ("not ... made
available 'as is'"), not CC0 or CC BY, and the mirror's CC BY-SA would also rule out shipping.
**Analysis and measurement are allowed** ("free to use these samples as you wish").
