# HSD Düsseldorf string-quartet recording (Mozart K465) with separated stems for each instrument

- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3601097, "Streichquartett-Aufnahme zur Verwendung in der
  Virtuellen Akustik", by D. Leckschat and C. Epe, Hochschule Düsseldorf, 2020-02-20.
- **Access date:** 2026-11-24
- **Licence:** the Zenodo licence field is `cc-by-4.0`.
- **What was taken:**
  - `Dokumentation_HSD Streichquartett_V1.1.pdf` (its MD5 matches Zenodo);
  - from `Streicherquartett_Complete_Project.zip`, by range requests with CRC-32 checked: every
    phrase and quartet folder in `Audiodateien/` (one stem per instrument plus `ref` mixes), the
    four `Reference_Level_*_94dBSPL.wav`, `Messungen/` (microphone and equipment list, a
    reverberation and level PDF) and `Zeichnungen/` (microphone and musician position drawings).
- **Not taken:** the loudspeaker sweeps for each position, the room-silence files, the photos and
  the score.

## Content, from the record description (verbatim excerpt)

> Sie umfasst neben voneinander akustisch separierten Einzelsignale Referenzmischungen des kompletten Werkes sowie einzelne Phrasen ausgewählter Streichinstrumente.

That is: besides acoustically separated single signals, it contains reference mixes of the whole
work and single phrases of selected string instruments.

- **Recording method (documentation):**
  - A guide track was recorded first. Then **each instrument was recorded separately** over the
    guide track.
  - Spot microphones: Sennheiser MKH 800. Main microphones: Schoeps MK2S.
  - Levels were calibrated to a reference measurement microphone (Behringer ECM8000), with a
    94 dB SPL reference tone for each instrument.
  - Recorded in the large studio's recording room at HSD.
- **Excerpts:** Mozart K465, Andante cantabile.
  - Quartet Long: bars 75-114, 2:21.
  - Quartet Phrases 1-3: bars 8-12, 26-31 and 81-84.
  - Violin Phrases 1-3.
  - Cello Phrases 1-3.
- **Stems:** `Vl1`, `Vl2`, `Vla` (**solo viola stems**), `Vlc`.
- **Format:** WAV, mono, 24-bit, 48 kHz. For example, `Quartet_Long_Vla.wav` is 141 s.
- **Files:** 36 WAV files plus documents, 200 MB.
- **Viola material:** `Quartet_Long_Vla` and `Quartet_Phrase_{1,2,3}_Vla`, all real legato and
  vibrato playing. The only viola stems are the quartet ones; there are no viola-only phrases.
- **Instruments and players:** not named. They are independent of everything else here.

## Verdict

**CC BY 4.0: it may ship with attribution** (Leckschat, Epe, Hochschule Düsseldorf). Analysis
is allowed. The spot microphones are separated acoustically, so expect some bleed from the guide
track and the room. This is the verified CC BY **solo viola** recording asked for in item 8.
