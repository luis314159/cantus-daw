# Mores & Plath 2018: optically measured torsional waves on a bowed cello string (traces only)

- **URL:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1302895 (https://zenodo.org/records/1302895).
  This is reference 22 of PLOS ONE doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0211217.
- **Access date:** 2026-11-23
- **Licence:** the Zenodo licence field is `cc-by-4.0`.

## Content, from the record's description (verbatim excerpts)

> Measurements by a high speed camera at 7000 frames per second, on axis with the bowed string
> Transverse: optical marker on the string (transverse), 70 mm away from the bridge, 10 mm away from the bow contact point.
> Torsional: optical marker at each end of two needles (top, and bottom, each 4mm long, total weight including marker 0.002 g)
> bowing at the target contact point 80 mm away from bridge [...] For each level (high, medium, low) the player adjusted bow velocity reasonably.
> Tables in *.mqa format hold pixel row and column of extracted optical marker (top,transverse, botteom) for respective bow force (3=medium, 5=low,6=high).

- **String:** Pirastro Chromcor medium G. Properties: 6.15 g/m, 1.19 mm, 121 N, v_tra 133 m/s,
  Z0 0.93 kg/s, torsional fundamental 543 Hz, v_tor 738 m/s.
