In one paragraph

Cantus is a free, open-source digital audio workstation written in Rust and drawn by hand, in the tradition of FL Studio's workflow. It has an arrangement, a piano roll, a channel rack and a mixer; nine instruments and twenty-five effects of its own; hosting for CLAP and VST3 plug-ins; and export to WAV, FLAC, MP3 and Ogg. It needs no account and has no telemetry; the application is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later and its engine MPL-2.0. It is in public beta for Windows and Linux, with a macOS preview. Created by Luis Ángel Almazán.

In one line

A free, open-source DAW that helps you finish the song.
