Finding and hearing presets

Back to the manual · Leer en español

Every instrument and effect of Cantus comes with factory presets, and you can save your own. The preset browser finds them by what they are for and lets you hear one before you choose it — nothing in your song changes until you load it.

Open it

  • In an instrument's window, click the preset name in its title bar ▸ Browse presets… (the menu's first row).
  • In an effect's window, the same: its preset name ▸ Browse presets….

The browser opens beside the window, on that instrument's or effect's presets. Drag its title to move it; Esc or × closes it.

Find

  • Type to search: every word must be found in the preset's name, its style, its role or the unit's name — capitals and accents do not matter (electrico finds Eléctrico).
  • STYLE chips: trap · hip-hop, pop, house · EDM, R&B, lo-fi, rock, latin · reggaeton, ambient · cinematic, film · game, jazz · soul, worship · gospel. Each shows how many presets it has. Several chips are alternatives (trap or lo-fi).
  • ROLE chips: for an instrument the part it plays (bass, lead, pad, keys, pluck, effect, drums); for an effect where it goes (vocal, drums, bus, master, creative). Style and role together narrow the list (a trap bass). Click a chip again to turn it off.

Hear

  • Click ▶ on a row: Cantus renders the preset and plays it once. An instrument is heard on a short phrase for its role — a bass line, a melody, held chords, comping, an arpeggio, a drum groove — at 120 BPM, or at your song's tempo with Sync BPM on. An effect is heard on the clip you selected (its track alone), or on the first clip its channel plays; with no clip, on a short source Cantus makes with its own instruments.
  • ↑ ↓ choose the previous or next preset and play it; Space plays the chosen one again or stops it.
  • The row keeps a small waveform of what you heard; the player at the bottom shows the large one, what it was heard on, and how many presets the filters leave.

What you hear is rendered by Cantus, with no processing after it. Your project, its undo history and its file are never touched by hearing a preset.

Load

Double-click a row, press Enter, or click Load: the preset is loaded into the instrument or effect as one step of undo (Ctrl+Z gives the old sound back). A gold dot marks the preset loaded last.
