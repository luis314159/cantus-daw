Fides, the resonator

Cantus 0.1.0-beta.17 · Leer en español · back to the manual

Fides makes sound the way acoustic instruments do: something excites an object — a finger plucks it, a pick snaps it, a bow drags it, a mallet strikes it, noise blows on it — and the object resonates: a string, a bar, a tine, a plate, a membrane, a tube, a bell, a glass. A body radiates what it rings with. Nothing is sampled: every note is computed from the physics as you play, so it answers to how hard, where and how long you play it, and it never repeats a recording. Marimba, vibraphone, kalimba, steel pan, harp, nylon guitar, a bowed cello and glass are its first presets; the family goes further (bells, gongs, timpani, a hammered dulcimer, a pan flute, bowed pads, rain on glass).

Its name is the Latin for the strings of a lyre — a stringed instrument — and for faith.

Add it from the device picker (the + above the track headers, its Keys shelf, and Other instruments). A new Fides plays a plucked nylon string in a guitar's body.

The Play view

The picture (left) is the instrument as it is set: the resonator drawn as a plan — a string between its bridge (left) and its nut, a bar with the nodes of its first mode dashed, a tine from its clamp, a plate, a drumhead seen from above with the radius it is struck along, a tube (closed at the right when it is one), a bell, a glass, a pan — the exciter drawn where it plays it, and the microphone's ring where a modal object is heard from. Drag across it to move where it is played: the partials below follow.

Under it, the partials: each a line at its frequency over the fundamental (×1, ×2, ×4 … on a log axis), as tall as the strike excites it where it is played. A string's are its harmonics with the gaps the position cuts (play it at a fifth of the length and every fifth harmonic is gone); a bar's are its stretched modes (×2.76, ×5.40 …); a marimba bar's are tuned to two octaves and three octaves and a third. While a note sounds they light with what the newest voice rings.

Position is where it is played, as a fraction of the length from the end the picture draws at the left: a string's bridge, a bar's end, a tine's tip, a membrane's rim, a bell's lip. Near the end is thin and bright; the middle is round. Noise lays the noise generator under any exciter (a bow's rosin, a mallet's felt).

Decay is how long the fundamental rings (T60, the time to fall 60 dB). Material is how the highs die: wood's die at once, glass's and metal's ring as long as the fundamental. Inharmonicity stretches (or squeezes) the partials. Listen is where a modal object is heard from (the microphone's ring).

The body: None, Guitar (its air mode near 98 Hz and its top's modes), Violin (its signature modes and the bridge hill near 2.5 kHz), Cello, Harp (its soundbox and soundboard), Box (a kalimba's), Shell (a drum's or a pan's skirt) — with its mix, size (a bigger body lowers its modes) and ring.

Output: Release (how fast the resonators are damped when you let go: 60 s lets them ring as a marimba's bars do), Width (voices spread across the stereo by key) and Gain.

The Edit view

Every part as a card: the picture and the exciter; Resonator A and Resonator B (B's lamp turns it on), each with every knob — Key decay (how much faster a note an octave up dies), Partials (how many modes a modal object plays: fewer is lighter on the CPU), Tune and Fine (B against A), Key track (0 % holds the resonator's pitch whatever the key: a drum); the coupling — Parallel (the exciter into both) or A into B (A drives B, as a marimba's bar drives the tube under it) and what is heard of each; the bow and MPE; the noise with its envelope; the body; vibrato (depth in cents, rate, a delay before it starts) and tremolo (its phase the song's clock, as a vibraphone's motor); the output with its low and high cuts; the voice — Poly, Mono or Legato, Strike again (on: a key struck while it rings strikes the same resonator again, as a real bar or string; off: a new voice), Voices, Glide, Bend range, Octave, Transpose, Fine, A4, Velocity, Release and the Seed of its noise.

Playing it

  • Velocity sets how hard the strike is: louder and, by Velocity tone, a shorter contact (brighter), as a faster mallet's is.
  • MPE: a note's pressure presses the bow (MPE pressure: how much; with Noise or Input it raises the level), its slide moves where it is played (MPE slide), its pitch bend bends it. A non-MPE controller's aftertouch, wheel and expression are modulation sources.
  • Pitch is exact: the waveguide's loops and filters are solved for the fundamental, and the modes are set by their angles — measured within 0.002 cent at every bend.
  • The sustain pedal holds released notes, as a vibraphone's damper bar.

What it costs

A plucked or bowed string is a few dozen operations a sample; a modal object one complex multiplication a partial (48 at most). Its voices split across the engine's threads with the bits of one (ADR 0038). The Partials knob is the price of a modal voice.
