## Briefs

### record (ADR 0092) — the §4c decisions of 2026-10-10 are binding
Read BACKLOG §4c whole, ADR 0064, 0005, 0022, `freeze_guard.rs`. Order: (1) **tests first** —
null (baked + summed equals normal render to the bit with controls untouched, mix controls
included), block-size independence at tolerance 0, offline = real time, parallel = serial,
delay compensation keeps strips aligned, a changed strip invalidates exactly its own render, a
crashed session reopens out of the mode, `rt_memory` zero allocations in the mode's path, **a
new Command fails the build until classified** (reuse `touches()`). (2) Low-latency monitoring
for armed strips (constrain delay compensation: the live path is not held back for latent
plug-ins elsewhere, or those are bypassed and named on screen; the take still placed to the
sample). (3) The mode: *Prepare to record* renders every strip that feeds the master (a bus
is one render including what it sums; returns bake their sends) to f32 files keyed by the
strip hash, streamed by a reader thread with a prefetch ring (no I/O on the audio thread);
armed strips stay live; master live or baked, mix controls live or locked; everything else
greyed with "Recording mode — leave it to edit"; a banner that cannot be missed (name, buffer,
measured round trip, CPU before/after, strips baked, **Leave**, keep or discard files);
hosted plug-ins that cannot render offline are named and stay live or stop the preparation;
size shown before it starts; re-entering renders only changed strips. The state is the
session's, not the project's. **Honest wording** about what it buys (it does not shorten the
interface's round trip). Benchmark: 64 tracks with effects, lowest stable buffer, before/after
CPU and dropouts, in `docs/round36/record.md`. Agents: `prepare_to_record`, `leave_recording_mode`,
`get_recording_mode`. Manual EN/ES.

### mix (ADR 0093, schema 50)
VCA: a strip kind with no audio whose fader scales the gain of its members at the **gain
stage, not the fader value** (members' own faders and automation stay intact and visible; the
VCA's own automation is a gain curve) — define precisely in the ADR how nested VCAs, mute/solo
following and a member in two VCAs behave and test each; groups that use a VCA; null test
(a VCA at 0 dB touches no sample). Snapshots: capture all or selected channels' mixer state
(fader, pan, mute, solo, sends, inserts' bypass/parameters as the ADR decides, EQ), recall all
or per channel, with a *safe* set; automatable as scenes (a snapshot recalled by a clip or a
bar, undoable as one step); compare/A-B. Console feel: what FL and the consoles list, decided
in the ADR. Agents: `create_vca`, `assign_vca`, `take_snapshot`, `recall_snapshot`. Undo fuzz,
`cmd.*` strings, fixture `v50.cantus`, manual EN/ES, shots of the mixer in both themes.

### external (ADR 0094, 0095, schema 51)
Units: *External Instrument* (MIDI out + audio in) and *External Effect* (audio out + in) with
interface outputs/inputs chosen, **an automatic latency ping** (a click/impulse out and back,
measured to the sample, stored per unit, re-measurable, said on screen) feeding PDC; recorded
and bounced results placed to the sample; offline render uses the stored latency; null/timing
tests with `loopback.rs`; a unit whose interface is unplugged is said, never loaded blindly.
Surfaces: Mackie Control (MCU, extenders, motor faders with touch, V-Pots, transport, jog,
banks/channels, scribble strips and the 7-segment display, meters, flip, assignments) and HUI
(its ping/zone protocol), with an **emulation** (a virtual device pair and a test harness that
speaks both protocols byte for byte) so everything is tested without hardware; MIDI device
setup in two clicks; generic mapping stays. Agents: `add_external_unit`, `ping_external_unit`,
`list_surfaces`. Mark in `docs/mac-session.md`/beta checklist what needs the owner's hardware.

### editor (ADR 0096, schema 52 if it must)
Edison-class editor opened from a clip (*Edit in audio editor*) and returning to it: a
destructive edit makes a new asset (content-addressed, the original kept until the project
is cleaned) and one undo step each; operations: cut/copy/paste/delete, silence, fade in/out
and curves, normalize (peak, RMS/LUFS), invert, reverse, gain, DC remove, **remove silence**,
**noise reduction by profile** (learn from a selection, spectral subtraction with smoothing,
deterministic, a null test at amount 0), **spectral view** (spectrogram, zoom, select a
time-frequency region, attenuate it), sample-accurate selections, markers. Tests: bit-exact
null for identity edits, undo exact, a cleaned take measurably quieter in noise-only frames
with speech-like content intact. Agents: `edit_audio`, `denoise_clip`. Shots in both themes.

### ara (ADR 0097)
Measure: Celemony's ARA SDK licence (is it compatible with a GPL-3.0 application and with
MPL engine crates? the Celemony developer terms, the need for a signed agreement), the cost,
the SDK's surface and what hosting requires of our plug-in host (`cantus-host`, `cantus-plugins`:
VST3 `IARA…` factory extension, audio sources/modifications/playback regions, region
rendering, the timeline and edit sync with our clips), which plug-ins implement ARA 2 to test
with (a free one if any), the work in rounds. Write the ADR with a go/no-go and, if go, the
design and the first slice; **report the decision to the integrator before writing code**. If
no-go, BACKLOG §3c/§0.4 say exactly why and what would change it.
