## How each was checked (2026-11-23)

beta.17 is round 41 (Fides, Nitor, Fragor's pedals, Chorda's strings in tune, the theme
following the system) on private master `e46ba7136`. Its candidate was published to cantus-daw
as the branch `rc/0.1.0-beta.17`, not to master (RELEASING step 1, new in this release): the
site deploys on every push to the public master, and it would have shown Fides's and Nitor's
cards for hours while beta.16 was the download. The tag goes on that branch; master follows
once the release is out.

- **L1:** run 45086843755, a hand-run (`workflow_dispatch`) of `release.yml` on
  `rc/0.1.0-beta.17` at c6c00660 (private e46ba7136). Every job is green: preflight, `test`
  (Windows), `package` (the installer), `linux (AppImage)` and `macos (disk image)`, with
  `CANTUS_RELEASE_MACOS` true. `publish` was skipped, as a rehearsal's is.
- **L2:** every `cargo test --workspace` step runs with `--no-fail-fast`. Step times against
  the jobs' limits, 2/3 of each in brackets: Windows 176 min of 330 (220); Linux 153 of 300
  (200), its whole job 216; macOS 146 of 300 (200), its whole job 184. Before it, the
  Windows suite on the owner's machine passed 8 318 tests (0 failed) on 7eb64c6ec, the same
  code but for the version, the notes, publish.sh and the site's head.
- **L3:** the release's new timing tests: `fx_fragor_pedal::cpu_of_each_pedal` compares each
  pedal with Fragor, best of five, and is ignored (it prints); `inst_fides`'s 32-voice test is

  it passed on all three runners; round 42 makes it the best of three. `fides_pick_e2e`'s
  120 s wait is a timeout, not a measure. The site budget's Lighthouse CPU is now calibrated
  to the runner (`web/tools/lhci_calibrated.mjs`): the gate that read 2 106 ms on a slow
