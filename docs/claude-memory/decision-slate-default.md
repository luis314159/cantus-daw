Round 39, Phase A: three whole-window directions were drawn (A Slate, B Graphite and gold, C Studio ink; F:\projects\daw-r39\dir-{a,b,c}, sheet F:\projects\daw-r39\review\tres-direcciones.png).

**The owner chose A, Slate, as the default theme** (2026-11-09).
- The owner asked whether looking like FL could cause legal trouble. My reading was low risk. Measured: FL's background is #253035 (teal-tinted) with panels at #34444E. Slate's base is #1D2329 and its surface #262D34, with an azure accent where FL uses orange/lime. The icons, type, logo and name are Cantus's own.
- The owner accepted it as the default. The legal review stays pending in docs/beta-checklist.md.
- The system: dir-a's README and system.png. Barlow Semi Condensed (OFL 1.4.08); accent #5CAFE9 (Night) / #1F6EC2 (Paper).

**Icons:** the first set (r39/icons) was not approved. The owner said: "vuelve a compararlos con FL y otros DAWs, creo que lo podemos hacer mejor, más elegantes, bonitos y mejor pensados". It is being redrawn against the Ableton, Bitwig, Logic and Studio One references in research/ui/screenshots/.

**How to apply:** ADR 0111 records Slate as the design system. B and C may become optional themes later (ask the owner). See [[decision-round39-ux-graphics]], [[feedback-more-agents-r39]].

**2026-11-09, later:**
- **Dancers out of the website and README.** The owner: "tengo dudas sobre los dancers … ya no va ni con el estilo". They stay in the app. BACKLOG §6j has "review and start again".

- **At the end of round 39:** update the website and the repo's screenshots with the new look.
- **Track icons.** The library grew to 316 in 17 families. The guitars have been rejected twice and are being redrawn from real photo references (r39/icons-guitars).
- **Cables.** Direction B (signal flow) is the default, with all three styles selectable (r39/el-cables).
- **Logo #03 ("the metal") is the default** (owner 2026-11-09, after seeing 4 enriched variants of #10 — kept on record in r39/logo). Small masters redrawn (m03/).
- **Lapis #7EA2F6 / #2443A8 is the WEBSITE accent only** (owner corrected 2026-11-09: "no planeaba cambios de colores de la app por lo del logo") — the app keeps Slate azure #5CAFE9 / #1F6EC2. Gold rejected as an accent (collides with solo amber). Website redesigned to match #03 (r39/web).
- **"Slate Lapis" theme** (lapis accent + gold hairline playhead/loop) added as a NEW optional theme (owner 2026-11-09); Slate stays the default unchanged.
