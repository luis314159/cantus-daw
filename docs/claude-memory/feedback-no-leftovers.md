The owner, round 26: "De los features que dejaron huecos pendientes hay que atenderlos para
hacerlos bien, es importante si no iremos dejando cosas después."

**Why:** rounds kept ending with "what is left" lists that rolled into later rounds and
piled up.

**How to apply:** after each builder's report and review, collect its "Left" items and send
them back as follow-ups in the same round (only owner-only items — hardware, paperwork,
decisions — may stay open, and they go to `docs/beta-checklist.md` as the owner's). Briefs
should ask builders to finish features end to end (e.g. a content pack must be installable
*and* playable). STATUS "The next session" should then hold only owner items and new work,
not leftovers. See [[goal-best-daw]], [[cantus-workflow-habits]].
