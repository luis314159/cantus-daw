Two owner decisions after round 28 (2026-10-16), both in BACKLOG (§3b and §3e) and STATUS:

1. **Effects sound when inserted (option B).** The round-28 modulation family enters at
   Mix/Depth 0 because of the null-at-defaults rule; the owner found that reads as "it does
   nothing". Chosen: inserting an effect loads a musical starting preset (e.g. Concentus's
   *Juno I*); the parameters' defaults stay a null, so ADR 0018 and its tests are unchanged.
   Option C (defaults that sound) was rejected because it breaks the family rule.
2. **Chorda "no suena bien la verdad".** Its measurements pass (tuning, decay, half pedal,
   CPU) but the owner's ear says no. Next piano work is sound quality by ear against
   references (Salamander in Exemplar, Pianoteq/sampled grands for the owner), before Chorda
   II's extras; ask the owner what exactly sounds wrong. Done when the owner says it sounds
   good.

**Why:** measurable targets alone produced an instrument that measures right and sounds
wrong; the owner judges by ear.

**How to apply:** in the next round's brief, make both items explicit; for any new
instrument, plan an ear-driven reference comparison, not only physics targets. See
[[round-28]], [[goal-best-daw]].

**Round 37 (2026-11-04), the owner's ear in detail:** Chorda "me sonaba sintético, muy
sintético, muy falso, nada real, sin cuerpo, muy fino" — synthetic, fake, no body, thin.
Model 2 aims at body (low-mid, soundboard radiation, bass weight, rich slowly beating
sustain), a real attack (thump, felt noise), seeded variation, and a room/perspective.

**2026-11-05/06, the owner's first ABX (round 37):** Chorda 2 preferred over Chorda 1 7/7; the
Salamander preferred over Chorda 2 7/7 (distinguished 15/16 both). His words: Salamander "más
vida, más bonitos graves y agudos"; Chorda 2 "muchos medios, algo borroso, un poco nasal por los
medios"; Salamander "más fuerte y vivo". Voicing pass two aimed at mids/honk, blur (body/room
smear, mic comb), lows/highs, crest factor/liveliness; new kit abx-kit2.
