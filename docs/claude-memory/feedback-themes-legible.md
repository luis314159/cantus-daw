Every shipped theme (24 in round 39: Slate family, the 10 restored, the new ones) must be easy to work in and to read, not only pretty. A theme keeps its character, but legibility wins.

**Why:** the owner, on seeing all themes side by side (2026-11-10): "Todos deben ser fáciles de trabajar y de leer con los diferentes temas".

**How to apply:** hold it with measured tests on rendered pixels across every theme. The bars are text at 4.5:1 or more, and non-text at 3:1: clip content on the clip body, selection, playhead, grid, caps, lit/unlit keys, meters and focus. The demo's track colours must also be told apart. Judge 100 % crops, not contact sheets. Related: [[ui-quality-bar]], [[decision-slate-default]].
