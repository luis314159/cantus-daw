The owner, 2026-11-20 (on the launch video's line 7, "No subscription. No paywall. Every
feature, for everyone."): **Cantus's own sound packs will always be free — always.** That does
not stop other parties (a third party) from offering paid packs of their own. The line stays.

**Why:** the free-forever promise (CLA §3, ADR 0047) covers content too; `docs/monetize.md`
(his, untracked) mentions paid packs — those are third-party, not Cantus's.
**How to apply:** never describe or plan a paid Cantus pack; copy may say every feature and
every Cantus pack is free. See [[decision-licensing-mpl-cla]], [[decision-cc-by-content]].
