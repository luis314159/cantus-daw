On 2026-11-25 the owner approved the string-sections design in `research/instruments-next/strings-sections-design.md` (commit bdfa57bd2 on r42/integration):
- **One Arcus with a Section choice** of 1–16 players, not separate picker entries. At N = 1 it is the solo Arcus, bit for bit.
- **Default size "Film":** 14/12/10/10/6.
- **Render:** Balanced by default (a body per desk group plus a per-player body-difference filter). Full is optional. The hybrid "Auto" only ships if it passes the ABX.
- **The shared project stage** (a schema change) goes in 43-B. 43-A covers the seeded players, true divisi and the stage through Tympanum's paths.

**Why:** the studies say a section needs about 4 or more distinct players (Ioannou & Kob 2019), and copies of one player phase.

**How to apply:** when writing round 43's contract, follow the study's phased plan (43-A first) and don't re-ask the owner these three choices.
