2026-10-10 the owner said "no problem citing — we accept that": CC BY 3.0/4.0 packs are
allowed besides CC0, with the credit always shown (Sound packs, About > Credits, the pack
receipt, a Content section in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES). NC, ND, SA and retired CC licences stay
out (SA only if the owner rules on a specific pack). Recorded in ADR 0042 (amends ADR 0001
and ADR 0030 §5).

**Why:** the best free libraries (Salamander Grand, Naked Drums, DrumGizmo, VocalSet) are
CC BY; CC0-only left the piano and acoustic drums without content.

**How to apply:** when adding packs, CC BY is fine if the manifest carries the credit and
licence file; the cantus-content manifest test still needs changing to accept CC-BY
(BACKLOG §5). See [[round-24]].
