Set by the owner on 2026-10-07: the final goal is for Cantus to be the best DAW in the world,
among free and commercial ones — "maybe not at first, but in the future" — in objective terms
(features, performance, quality, stability), so that it can *undeniably* be said to be the
best. Getting people to use it is a separate track worked on in parallel.

**Why:** the mission is excellence (Colossians 3:23); "best" must be provable, not claimed.

**How to apply:** `docs/BACKLOG-excellence.md` §0 holds the public scorecard (against FL,
Ableton, Bitwig, Logic, Cubase, Pro Tools, Studio One, Reaper, Ardour, LMMS…) and the gaps no
other section covered; judge plans against it, and keep the measurements reproducible. My
honest view given to the owner the same day: the foundations are first-rank; real use, the
beta and a community decide the rest — win the free/open-source space first. See
[[round-22]], [[feedback-backlog-ideas]].

Domains owned (2026-10-07): **cantusdaw.com** and **cantusdaw.studio** — the site and backend
plan is backlog §6a (downloads, manual, packs mirror, updates, user count without telemetry).
