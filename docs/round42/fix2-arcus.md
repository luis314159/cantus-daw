# Round 42 — `fix2-arcus`: the mid-round review's findings on Arcus's and Exemplar's DSP

Branch `r42/fix2-arcus` from `r42/integration` 1da2e8df6, merged up to 7c1d543a0. The nine
findings the integrated review confirmed for Arcus (ADR 0157) and Exemplar's scripted legato
(ADR 0158), each with a test that failed before its fix. Everything here was verified in
emulation and offline renders. No device or listening session was needed for these: they are
numbers, not timbre.

## The invariants

- **Null tests.** A default tuning and default controls change no sample. Every added term is
  `+ 0.0` or `× 1.0` at the default, and the body's power at the defaults is the old measure,
  summed in the same order.
- **Gates.** The whole `cantus-dsp` suite is green: 1 385 passed. That includes
  `upper_tests::the_cello_and_the_bass_keep_their_bits` (`LOWER_HASHES` unchanged), the
  bowed-pitch gates (168 + the held-out points, and the upper strings'), the attack gate, block
  sizes, the pool and rest. No golden hash moved and no `DSP_VERSION` bump was needed.
- **Engine.** `inst_arcus`, `arcus_image`, `tuning_instruments`, `strings_rec_art`,
  `rt_memory` and the undo fuzz all pass (counts in the report).
