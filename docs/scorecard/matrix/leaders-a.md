# Scorecard research — the leaders, part A (FL Studio, Ableton Live, Bitwig Studio, Logic Pro)

Researched **2026-11-04**. These features change with every release, so recheck the cells whenever the scorecard is refreshed.
Each cell rests on the vendor's own manual, release notes or product page, listed under Sources. `unverified` means no
vendor page confirmed the cell either way, and the cell gives the reason. `missing` means the feature is absent from the
vendor's current manual chapter or feature list for that area. It is a documentation finding, not a hands-on test.

Versions checked: **FL Studio 2026 (26.1.7.5, 2026-10-12)**, **Ableton Live 12.4.16 (2026-10-30)**,
**Bitwig Studio 6.1.4** (6.1 released 2026-08-26), **Logic Pro for Mac 12.4** (release notes dated 2026-09-29).

## Notes

- **Unverified cells: 23 of 388** (FL 4, Ableton 3, Bitwig 6, Logic 10). O4 (determinism) is unverified for all four, because no vendor publishes a claim either way. Cantus can measure and publish this row; the leaders do not.
- **Some vendor pages could only be read in part.**
  - Apple's guide pages are mostly navigation text, so the Logic guide cells rest on Apple-domain search quotes.
  - Bitwig's "latest" user guide is still v5.3, so 6.x additions rest on the 6.0 and 6.1 release notes.
  - Seven Ableton cells (R2, W6, W8, I2, I5, L4, X7) rest on help or Packs pages read only through search.
  - Spot-check these cells before publishing.
- **Editions differ:**
  - **FL Studio:**
    - Stem separation, audio recording, Edison and Newtime are Producer and up.
    - Newtone, Pitcher and Gross Beat are Signature and up.
    - Harmor, Sakura, Kepler Exo, Transmitter and Emphasis are All Plugins only.
    - Some individual plug-in pages name a different edition from the compare page: Newtime and Newtone say "All Plugins Edition or purchase separately". These cells follow the compare page.
    - FL Cloud AI mastering and the 2-million-sound library need a paid FL Cloud subscription.
  - **Ableton Live:**
    - Suite alone has stem separation, Max for Live, Wavetable, Operator, Sampler, Meld, Granulator III, Hybrid Reverb and Surround Panner.
    - Audio to MIDI and the modulators need Standard.
    - The compare page's headline counts (8/12/21 instruments) disagree with its own lists (9/14/21).
  - **Bitwig Studio:** Essentials has 3 stretch modes and no comping. Producer has 8 modes and comping. Studio has 43 modulators and The Grid.
- **Surprises:**
  - **Real-time sync:** Logic cannot receive MIDI clock. Bitwig and Ableton handle MTC in one direction only (Bitwig sends, Ableton receives). FL Studio has no Ableton Link.
  - **Open formats:** FL and Logic have no DAWproject. Ableton has neither CLAP, ARA nor DAWproject. Logic has neither VST3 nor CLAP.
  - **FL Studio:**
    - FL has no MPE, no ARA (Image-Line forum staff: "on the list"), no comping, no freeze and no VCA. It is the only leader without a wavetable synth in its manual's generator list.
    - FL's AI offering is broad: stem extraction, AI declip and deverb, FL Cloud AI mastering, and Gopher, an in-app agent that can now change the project (experimental, no external API or MCP).
  - **Recent additions elsewhere:** Ableton added Auto Shift (12.1), take comping and Bounce in Place (12.2) and local stem separation (12.3). Bitwig 6.0 added automation clips, clip aliases and a tempo lane with curves.
- **Cells to double-check before publishing:**
  - **Logic:**
    - P8 says ARA 2 needs Rosetta on Apple silicon. It rests on Apple support article 102082, which may be out of date, so confirm it in the 12.x guide.
    - The tech-specs counts (28 instruments, 108 effects, 72 GB) and X8 (no FLAC) rest on a specs sheet that may be the Logic 11 one.
  - **FL Studio:**
    - T3 (tempo automation clip) is documented only in forum and third-party material.
    - R1: the manual documents no punch-in.
    - O2: prices are served by region, and the US$449 is a search snapshot.
    - A3/A4 rest on the manual's own terms (per-note events; Link to controller). How far "any parameter" reaches for third-party plug-ins is not stated.
  - **Bitwig:** P7 (delay compensation) rests on a release-notes snippet.
- **Marketing rather than documentation:**
  - Apple's product page calls Session Players, Stem Splitter, Chord ID, Mastering Assistant and Pitch Correction "AI", but the guide does not say how they work.
  - Image-Line's "over 2 million sounds" is the FL Cloud subscription library, not what ships with the DAW.
  - Bitwig says "over 140 devices"; its edition counts (51/105/188) include modulators and containers.
