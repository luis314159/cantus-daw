Your first ten minutes

1Pick your audio device

ASIO or WASAPI on Windows. The status bar shows the latency your driver reports, so you know what you are playing through.

2Open a starter or a demo

Six demo songs ship with it — open Neon Tide and press space. Every sound in it is Cantus's own.

3Make it yours

Draw a pattern in the channel rack, play it into the piano roll, mix it. The manual walks the whole path in English and Spanish.
