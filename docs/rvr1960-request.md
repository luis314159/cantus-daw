## English (to send)

**Subject:** Permission request – Reina-Valera 1960 verses in a free music application (Cantus)

Dear American Bible Society Licensing Team,

My name is Luis Ángel Almazán, and I am the creator of **Cantus**, a free digital audio
workstation (software for recording and producing music) for Windows, with macOS and Linux
versions planned. Cantus is free of charge, has no advertising and requires no account. Its
website is https://cantusdaw.com.

Cantus is made with Christian values. When the program starts, it greets the user with one
short Bible verse about music, praise or work done with all one's heart (for example
Colossians 3:23). The user can switch this off in the settings. For Spanish-speaking users
I would like to show these verses in the **Reina-Valera 1960**, and I am writing to ask for
your permission, because your terms ask for written permission for applications.

The details of the use:

- **Number of verses:** 293 verses (281 references), never more than 300. The list is
  attached. No book is quoted beyond about 10 % of its verses (Psalms 115 of 2,461;
  Philippians 10 of 104), and the verses are a very small part of the program.
- **How they appear:** one verse at a time, on the start screen and in a small card when
  the program opens, with its reference followed by "RVR1960". The verses are never sold,
  never used as song lyrics, and the Reina-Valera 1960 logo is not used.
- **Credit:** the program's About window shows: *"Reina-Valera 1960 © Sociedades Bíblicas
  en América Latina, 1960. Renovado © Sociedades Bíblicas Unidas, 1988. Utilizado con
  permiso."* I will use any other wording you prefer.
- **Non-commercial:** Cantus is distributed free of charge. The project may accept
  voluntary donations, and may in the future offer optional sound packs for sale; the
  verses would never be part of anything sold.
- **Open source:** the program's source code is published under the GNU General Public
  License, which allows others to redistribute it. The verse text is kept in a separate
  data file under your notice, not under that licence, and our documentation states that
  anyone redistributing Cantus may not include the Reina-Valera 1960 text without your
  permission. Until permission is granted, Cantus uses the public-domain Reina-Valera 1909.

Could you please let me know whether this use is permitted, and under which conditions
(notice wording, any fee or report)? If it helps, I would also like to show the same
verses on the project's website under the same terms.

Thank you very much for your work and for considering this request.

Kind regards,

Luis Ángel Almazán
