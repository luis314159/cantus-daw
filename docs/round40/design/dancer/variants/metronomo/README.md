# Metrónomo explorations: stopped

The owner first wrote *"Metrónomo no me gustó nada"*, then corrected himself: *"Me he
equivocado, Metrónomo está chido, Onda es el que no me gusta."* The butler Metrónomo stays in
the cast as it was (`../../metronomo-*`).

These two explorations had been started before the correction. They are kept as they were,
unpolished, with no sheets or GIFs:

- `metronomo_m1.py`: **Clic**, a digital metronome. A slab device with an LED-segment face,
  a live 7-segment BPM, a beat light and a TAP pad. Tick-tock robotics; signature: a tempo
  ramp that breaks loose.
- `metronomo_m2.py`: **Péndulo**, the acrobat. A gymnast in a one-hand handstand on a small
  metronome housing, her body the inverted pendulum. Signature: double-time swings into a
  flip and a stuck landing.

They import the design rig from `../../src/`. To look at one, copy it back into `src/` and run
`python try_poses.py metronomo_m1 jump`.
