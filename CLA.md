> **Draft for legal review** (ADR 0047). It is written in plain terms on purpose; it will

Every contribution accepted into Cantus **will always remain available under the licence
it was contributed under** — GPL-3.0-or-later for the application, MPL-2.0 for the engine
crates (see `LICENSE`). The Steward may *also* offer Cantus under other terms (for

contribution available *only* under terms that are not free. Cantus cannot be closed
with the community's work.

- The contribution is your original work, or you have the right to submit it under this

- You are not required to support your contribution; it is given "as is", without
  warranties.

## 5. How you agree

Tick the box *"I agree to the Cantus Contributor Licence Agreement 1.0"* in your pull
request (the template has it). Your agreement covers that contribution and every later
one, until you tell us in writing that it no longer covers future contributions.
